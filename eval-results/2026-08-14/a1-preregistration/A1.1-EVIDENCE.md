```
   B loses 2 of 5 reps on 2 questions       -0.067         -0.267

   B wrong on all 5 reps of 1 question      -0.125         -0.500
```

**Two findings against RESULTS §10.4**, both from computing it rather than reading it:

1. Its table is the `acc = 1.00` **column** of surface A, labelled "the best 90% lower bound each
   n can produce". It is the **worst** perfect tie. At `acc = 0.50` a perfectly concordant pair
   set returns a **zero-width** interval at every n, so "at 30 pairs the bar fails at a perfect
   tie, and no data could clear it" is false as stated.
2. The pairs were **pseudo-replicated**: `paired2x2` pairs on `(question, rep)`, turning 24
   questions into 120 "pairs". A 5× inflated n narrows the CI, and a narrow CI makes
   non-inferiority **easier** to declare — anti-conservative in the one direction a
   non-inferiority test must not lean.

Margin choice, from surface B: −0.05 is failed by one question losing two of five reps (−0.050)
and by a net-zero exchange (−0.050); −0.10 absorbs both and still **fails** the case the bar
exists to catch, one whole question lost (−0.125); −0.15 would swallow that too and is rejected.
Per repo, six questions cannot support the test at any margin worth stating (−0.200), so the
study has **one pooled** endpoint and per-repo figures stay descriptive with the unseen repo first.

Net: margin loosened 2×, effective sample tightened 5×. Both stated in DESIGN, neither hidden.

### 3.2 Three defects the build caught and reading would not have

1. **A green build that built nothing.** The repo declares a type named `Project`; a whole-word
   pass rewrote every `<Project Path=...>` in the `.slnx` into `<Jasteakplai Path=...>`. msbuild
   reported **"Build succeeded" in 0.33 s** on a solution containing zero projects. Fixed with a
   reduced key set (`MSBUILD_RESERVED`) inside build files and paths; C# still gets the full set.
2. **Cross-solution identity.** Upstream bundles three independent solutions. `LoggingBehavior`
   and `ServiceConfig` are *declared* in two of them and arrive from the `Ardalis.SharedKernel`
   **package** in the third, so a repo-wide "what this repo declares" sweep renamed package types
   at their use sites — `CS0246` × 2. Fixed by `--include`, scoping the extract to one solution.
3. **Aspire's underscore form.** `Projects.Clean_Architecture_Web` is generated with dots turned
   into underscores and a trailing `_Web` that denies a word boundary — matched by no dotted,
   spaced or concatenated key. Needs a leading-boundary-only pass.
