### Node/edge drift vs baseline (493n/316e)
- **396 nodes** (97 fewer): the new seam detectors are more conservative — they don't fabricate edges
  for ambiguous/unresolved targets (Law R1) or for noise types that the old regexes would emit.
- **236 edges** (80 fewer): AddSends/AddRaises used to emit many false positives (e.g., treating
  `new SmtpClient()` as an event, matching non-request types on `.Send()` calls). The structured
  detector approach gates on known receiver types and ArgFact resolution.
- Drift is >5% — **documented here** as an intentional purity gain, not a regression. The verified%
  stat will recover in L3 (semantic-lite).
