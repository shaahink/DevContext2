## What was measured, not read off a comment

**The entries-only scope was one lookup.** `BuildSections` has called `_query.ResolveEntry(focus)`
since G1.2 (`ContextPackBuilder.cs:750`) and has built a symbol-rooted `usage` section since then
(`:802`). `BuildMulti` resolved card entry ids through `FindEntry`, which scans `_snapshot.Entries`
and nothing else. So a Studio card naming a type produced **no focus → no sections → dropped** as
`"<type> (<title>): no content for its entries — omitted"`. The audit's §3.C reading was right and
the fix is a convergence, not new machinery.

**`usage` was built and unreachable.** The section existed for every symbol root; no entry in
`CardTypeSections` named it, so no card could ask for it. Reachable from `get_context`, unreachable
from the Studio — the two-pipelines-one-claim defect, in one table row.

**A new defect the new gate caught (pre-existing).** A card naming ONE focus under TWO spellings had
its section concatenated **into itself**: doubled bytes, doubled `Verified`/`Approx`, and the pack
paid tokens for both copies. Found by `Declared_entries_and_symbols_share_one_resolution` asserting
exactly one `_provenance:` footer. Closed with a per-card `focusesUsed` set.

**The D-G row collision is real at the picker's budget.** eShop's five
`OrderStatusChangedTo{AwaitingValidation,Paid,Shipped,StockConfirmed,Cancelled}IntegrationEventHandler`
consumers: `middleEllipsis(title, 26, 'head')` keeps `text.slice(0, 20)` + `…` + `text.slice(-5)`,
and all five share `OrderStatusChangedTo` (20 chars) and `ndler` (5 chars) exactly. S10 fixed the
*tail* collision and left this one standing — pinned now by a spec that asserts the collision
(`new Set(primaries).size === 1`) and then that the row is distinguishable once it carries the
target member.

**The C-3 empty state was a half-fix.** "Pick types from the omnibox above" pointed at an omnibox
that searched **entries only** — on the one repo where that sentence appears (a library with zero
entries) it could not comply. The omnibox now searches both tabs, and the sentence names the Types
tab, which exists.
