## Resolution rule (why a pin survives a re-analyze)

Seeds resolve **by `step.focus` against the live `session.entryGroups()`**, and the card takes the
resolved entry's `nodeId` and `title` — never the pinned step's. This is the same invalidation
principle N1.1 applied to cards (ids are addressed in a graph; the graph is the thing that
invalidates them), applied at the point where a pin outlives its graph. A pin whose focus is gone
is counted and reported, not dropped in silence.

Every step **kind** resolves now, not just `entry`: a `node` step carries the focus of the trace it
was explored under (`workbench-page.ts:255`), so a pinned graph node seeds the flow it belongs to.
Pre-N1.2 the loop kept `kind === 'entry'` only, so pinning a node was worth exactly nothing. A
`reroot` step carries no focus by construction and is reported as unresolved.

## Side-finding, filed not fixed (run bug #3)

`ui/icon/icon.ts` renders an **empty span** for any name its `REGISTRY` does not carry — the effect
ends `if (!node) return`, silently. The app binds five such names; `bookmark` and `history` were
added here because the seed button binds them, leaving `box`, `edit`, `grip-vertical`, `lock` still
drawing nothing. Found because `<app-icon name="history">` on the very button this checkpoint
rewrites has been invisible since it shipped.
