## L3.3 Delivered — verified-edge ratchet & body-facts semantic upgrading

### Approach

Three-part upgrade strategy:

1. **Expanded UpgradeBodyFacts** (SemanticLitePopulator.cs) — added `CreationOp.Type` and
   `InvocationOp.GenericArgs` semantic binding via the merged compilation. Two new helpers:
   `TryBindCreationType` and `TryBindGenericArg`.

2. **Semantic-location index in AddSeamsFromDetectors** (GraphBuilder.cs) — built a provenance→
   (file:line) and body-level index of all Semantic-tier SymbolRefs from upgraded BodyFacts.
   Used during edge creation to upgrade Syntactic seam edges to Semantic when the target
   short name matches a verified SymbolRef.

3. **Semantic check in AddCallEdges** (GraphBuilder.cs) — same body-facts index, matching
   CallEdge callee FQNs against Semantic SymbolRef short names via suffix match.

4. **CallEdge upgrade via merged compilation** (SemanticLitePopulator.UpgradeCallEdges) —
   re-resolves Syntactic CallEdges against the full compilation with NuGet refs.

### What moved: 7 ReadsWrites edges Syntactic → Semantic

The provenance-index approach matched IdentifierUseOp → EntityTouchDetector edges where
the LocalDeclOp at the same file:line had a Semantic-tier InferredFrom. These are entity-
touch patterns in bodies that also declare locals with verifiable types.

### What did NOT move (and why)

- **Sends (32/33 approx):** MediatR dispatch edges are anchored on lambda bodies. The
  SemanticLitePopulator processes method bodies from BodyFactsExtractor, but dispatch
  lambdas are inlined and their facts are extracted by AddLambdaSeams at graph-build
  time — they never see the Tier B compilation. To fix: extract lambda BodyFacts during
  the BodyFactsExtractor pass so the populator can upgrade them.

- **Calls (30/121 approx):** CallEdges from CallGraphExtractor use a per-file compilation
  (TPA refs only). The merged compilation with NuGet refs should resolve more, but
  re-resolving post-hoc only upgraded 1 edge (method bodies that lost bind). The real fix
  is to reorder the pipeline: build the merged compilation before Stage3Specific extractors
  and pass it to CallGraphExtractor for its semantic resolution pass.
