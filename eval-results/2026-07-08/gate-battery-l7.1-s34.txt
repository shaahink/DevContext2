== What changed ==
Created PlainCallDetector (Graph2/Seams/PlainCallDetector.cs), a new ISeamDetector
that emits EdgeKind.Calls seams for every InvocationOp whose receiver type resolves to
an in-solution type via the SymbolTable. Framework types (ILogger, IMapper, HttpClient,
etc.) are automatically excluded because they don't resolve. MediatR/bus/domain-event
verbs are skipped to avoid redundant parallel edges with existing specialised detectors.

Registered in both AddSeamsFromDetectors and AddLambdaSeams detector arrays in
GraphBuilder.cs, with a 0.5f confidence (syntactic tier, upgradeable by
SemanticLitePopulator to Semantic).
