  candidates instead of zero-shaped success; config-miss lists real keys; trace/get_context

  cold agent omitting handle hits our envelope, not the SDK's opaque bind error.

  envelope (nearest tool by prefix/substring/Levenshtein + full tool list) for unknown tools.
