# T4.2 — Budget utilization ≥85% (spine-first body expansion, +N-lines markers)

**Date:** 2026-07-16 · **Branch:** feat/tapestry-t4

## What changed

The audit's Q7 pack used 612/4000 tokens. Three causes, three fixes in `ContextPackBuilder`:

1. **Bodies fill the remainder.** `BuildBodiesToFill(trace, remaining)` replaces the salient-only
   bodies section: spine-first (breadth-first — closest to the entry first), each step ships its
   FULL declaration text when that fits (member text recovered via the same
   `TraceBuilder.FindMemberDeclarationText` lookup the salient snippet uses, now internal;
   per-body cap = 2/5 of the fill so one god-class can't eat the pack), else the salient snippet
   with a visible `… (+N lines)` marker, else it's counted in
   `omitted[] = "bodies: N member bodies omitted (budget) …"`. Bodies always come LAST in every
   intent mode — structural sections first, bodies take what's left.
2. **The spine scales with the budget.** `BuildSections` traced at depth 4 — dogfood's checkout
   spine is 46 steps at depth 6 but ~12 at depth 4, so the fill ran out of content at 53%.
   Now: depth 6 when budget ≥3000 (or review intent), fan-out 16 when ≥6000.
3. **Structural sections are capped** (the plan's trap: "sections stay structural, bodies are
   where tokens go" — shamshir at depth 6 grew signatures to 2475/4000). Trace skeleton: shaped
   via T3.3 `ShapeToBudget` only when it exceeds `max(300, 15%)` (shaping an already-fitting
   skeleton over-cuts, since ShapeToBudget estimates WITH salient text). Signatures: BFS to
   `max(400, 25%)` with a named remainder `… (+N more members — raise budgetTokens …)`.
