## Attribution — the wall growth has ONE name

The bench's extractor columns account for ~10% of the big-repo walls. The T7.3 stage rows say
where the rest goes — DntSite Map, cold (`dntsite-stats-t7.2.txt`):

| Stage | ms | share |
|---|--:|--:|
| Discovery + Stage2 + Stage3 + sealing | 12370 | 13% |
| **SemanticLite** | **81096** | **86%** |
| GraphAssembly + Insights + Compression + Snapshot | 1165 | 1% |
| **Total** | **94740** | |

Same shape on shamshir (21.2s of 45.9s) and, by subtraction, OrchardCore (~30s of 42s). The
June-era engine parts (extraction + graph) still sum to ~13s on DntSite — **there is no silent
regression in the old pipeline**; the growth is the L3.1/L3.3 Tier-B semantic-lite tier
(whole-solution merged compilation + `UpgradeCallEdges` binding over every tree with full BCL
refs), which did not exist in the June baseline and scales with repo size. It is the same
"declaration table over the whole compilation" wall the 2026-07-01 investigation proved for
CallGraphExtractor — paid a second time by the upgrade pass.

Counter-signal proving the point: **CallGraphExtractor's own bind IMPROVED massively** —
DntSite Trace bind 84.3s (June) → 3.2s (entry/focus-scoped binding held); OrchardCore bind
5.1s ≈ the 4.3s the Jul-1 note measured. Small repos (≤150 files) are all ≤1.2s end-to-end —
the tier is cheap when the compilation is small.

## Named lever (recorded, deliberately NOT fixed in T7.2)

The one big lever is the known P3 from the Jul-1 investigation: **persist/reuse the merged
compilation** (cache by path+mtime; amortize across re-runs and interactive sessions), or scope

the extension — the attribution above came from CLI `--stats`), so future PERF files self-attribute.
