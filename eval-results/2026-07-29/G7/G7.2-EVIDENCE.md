# G7.2 — C-3: withhold, don't suppress, wherever a surface has no entries

**Checkpoint:** G7.2 · **Stage:** G7 · **Branch:** `feat/graph-v2` · 2026-07-29
**Strand doc:** `docs/dev/research/DECISIONS.md` §D-C (C-3), governed by the S9 scope correction.

---

## 1. Acceptance

C-2 (G7.1) proved the rule on one page. C-3 asks whether the product keeps it **wherever** a surface
has no entries. Done means:

1. Every route driven on a library pole, with **what the reader actually sees** captured — not just
   the sections a heuristic knows to look for.
2. Every entries-keyed violation found is either fixed or named with a reason it was not.
3. Each fix withholds (states why) rather than suppresses, per S9.
4. eShop canary: nothing that filled before withholds now.
5. The decisions are unit-tested in the battery, not only observed through a DOM probe.

## 2. The sweep

`scripts/g72-withhold-sweep.mts <repo> <out>` walks `/ · /explore · /atlas · /insights · /context ·
/mcp`, and per route records: full page text, every `[data-withheld]` with its reason class, every
*other* leaf that reads like an empty state, and the page's rendered character count (a page that is
near-blank on a library is the S9 defect itself, and a sweep that only reads existing sections
cannot see it).

Before: `g72-withhold-sweep-fluentvalidation.txt` — 5 marked withheld (all from G7.1's Atlas work),
7 unaudited.

**Two genuine violations, both entries-keyed, both found only by reading the full text:**

1. **The Context Studio's scope picker told an analyzed repo to go analyze a repo.**
   `totalEntryCount() === 0` rendered *"Analyze a repo to see its services and entries."* — on
   FluentValidation, which had just been analyzed. **Zero entries is not no analysis.** The component
   could not tell the two apart because it only received `entryGroups`; it had no idea whether a
   session existed. The same false sentence was also the disabled preset button's tooltip.
2. **The Insights page rendered the word "Coverage" over nothing.** The bar is gated on
   `g.entries > 0`, so on any entry-less repo the label stayed and the body vanished — the S9 shape
   one step short of suppression.

## 3. What landed

- `scope-picker.ts` — takes `analyzed` and `isLibrary` from the Studio, and `scopePickerWithheld()`
  (a pure exported function, same convention as `presetSeedsFor`) returns the three distinct states:
  not analyzed → `not-computed`, the original sentence, which is now only shown when it is true;
  analyzed library → `archetype`, pointing at the surface a library *does* have; analyzed non-library
  with no entries → `archetype`, pointing at the omnibox.
- `insights-view.ts` — the coverage block states what coverage measures and why this repo has none,
  and points at the Confidence Ledger, which S9 made reachable on a library.
- `context-studio.spec.ts` — the `SessionStore` double gained `ready` / `mapResponse`. The Studio now
  reads two more session signals; the stub was incomplete against the real store. No assertion moved.
- `scope-picker.spec.ts` — 3 new cases, the load-bearing one being *an analyzed repo is never told to
  analyze a repo*.
- `g72-withhold-sweep.mts` — the empty-state heuristic gained a word boundary; `/^no/` was matching
  *"Nodes appearing in the most distinct flows…"*, the Hub radar's caption.

## 5. The remaining unmarked empty states, and why they are not this checkpoint's defect

The sweep still reports 7 unmarked on the library pole and 6 on eShop. Read, one at a time:

- `0 of 0 selected` · `0 cards` · `0 tok` · `Generators 0` — **counters**, not empty states. A count
  of zero is a fact, and it is the same fact on both poles.
- *"No cards yet. Pick items from the scope picker to build context."* and *"No cards to meter."* —
  the Studio's **workspace** empty states. The workspace is empty because the reader has not filled
  it yet; the message is true, actionable, and identical on eShop. Not entries-keyed.
- *"No agent calls yet — 2 UI-origin calls hidden."* — already states its reason, and appears on
  both poles.

None of these vary with the entry count, which is the property C-3 is about.

## 6. Boundary — what this sweep did NOT cover

The sweep drives the six routes **in their default state**. Surfaces that only exist after an
interaction were not driven: the Inspector's tabs, the node card, the peek panel, the entry browser's
filtered states. Three of those carry unmarked empty states in source (`inspector.ts:184,217`,
`node-card.ts:82`, `stage.ts:250`) and each reads plausibly, but *plausibly* is what this program
keeps finding to be wrong. **They are unmeasured, not cleared** — a follow-up should drive them.
