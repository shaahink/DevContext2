## ANSWER KEY (verified ground truth)
It is the .NET Aspire reference eShop: a distributed, multi-service e-commerce sample. The composition root is the Aspire AppHost, src/eShop.AppHost/Program.cs, which is the single place the topology is declared - it registers the backing resources (redis, a rabbitmq resource named 'eventbus', postgres with catalogdb/identitydb/orderingdb) and then nine projects via AddProject: identity-api (Identity.API), basket-api (Basket.API), catalog-api (Catalog.API), ordering-api (Ordering.API), order-processor (OrderProcessor), payment-processor (PaymentProcessor), webhooks-api (Webhooks.API), webhooksclient (WebhookClient) and webapp (WebApp, the Blazor storefront). Each of those has its own src/<name>/Program.cs. Services talk to each other two ways: HTTP/gRPC for request-response (WebApp -> Ordering.API, WebApp -> Basket.API over gRPC) and a RabbitMQ integration-event bus (src/EventBus, src/EventBusRabbitMQ) for asynchronous flows. src/ClientApp and src/HybridApp are MAUI clients and are not part of the AppHost graph.

## Not started by the AppHost

**ClientApp** (.NET MAUI, MVVM with `[RelayCommand]` view models, e.g. `src/ClientApp/ViewModels/CheckoutViewModel.cs:104`) and **HybridApp** (MAUI + Blazor WebView, `src/HybridApp/Components/Pages/Catalog/Catalog.razor:1`) are runnable apps but are **not** referenced by `eShop.AppHost` — its dependency list is exactly the nine services above. The remaining projects — `EventBus`, `EventBusRabbitMQ`, `IntegrationEventLogEF`, `Ordering.Domain`, `Ordering.Infrastructure`, `WebAppComponents`, `eShop.ServiceDefaults` — are libraries with no entry point of their own.
