It is the .NET Aspire reference eShop: a distributed, multi-service e-commerce sample. The composition root is the Aspire AppHost, src/eShop.AppHost/Program.cs, which is the single place the topology is declared - it registers the backing resources (redis, a rabbitmq resource named 'eventbus', postgres with catalogdb/identitydb/orderingdb) and then nine projects via AddProject: identity-api (Identity.API), basket-api (Basket.API), catalog-api (Catalog.API), ordering-api (Ordering.API), order-processor (OrderProcessor), payment-processor (PaymentProcessor), webhooks-api (Webhooks.API), webhooksclient (WebhookClient) and webapp (WebApp, the Blazor storefront). That is 9 project processes + 1 YARP proxy + 3 containers. Cross-cutting: every project calls `AddServiceDefaults()`/`AddBasicServiceDefaults()` from `src/eShop.ServiceDefaults/Extensions.cs:16-46` (OpenTelemetry, health checks, service discovery, resilient HTTP), and the AppHost injects `ASPNETCORE_FORWARDEDHEADERS_ENABLED` into every project (`src/eShop.AppHost/Extensions.cs:22-46`).

Optional, **off by default**: OpenAI/Azure OpenAI and Ollama resources are gated by `bool useOpenAI = false;` and `bool useOllama = false;` (`src/eShop.AppHost/Program.cs:77-87`), so no AI container starts unless you flip those.
